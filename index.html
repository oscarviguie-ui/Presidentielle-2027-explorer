Far left

Nathalie Arthaud

Lutte ouvrière

Lutte ouvrière spokesperson, on her fourth presidential run.

How they'd govern and pay for it · Not costed

Programme rests on expropriating large groups and planning production, rather than on budget trade-offs; no costing.

+
Economy & wages
  • Minimum wage (SMIC) at €2,200 net a month+
  • Index all wages, pensions and benefits on real price rises+
  • Ban layoffs and share available work with no loss of pay+
  • Expropriate shareholders without compensation, starting with oil and arms+
Tax & public finances
  • Refuses to make workers, retirees or tenants pay for state debt+
  • Targets the ~€270bn a year in public aid and exemptions to companies+
  • Lift business secrecy so large groups' accounts are public+
  • Requisition oil majors' windfall profits to lower pump prices+
Pensions
  • Large pension increases, indexed on real prices+
  • Opposes any later retirement age+
  • Return to retirement at 60 2022+
  • No pension below €2,000 a month 2022+
Energy & climate
  • Expropriate large groups to plan energy production and water use+
  • Coordinate production across countries rather than compete+
Immigration
  • Regularise all undocumented migrants+
  • Freedom of movement and settlement for everyone+
  • End deportations of undocumented people+
Security & justice

No documented position yet.

Europe & defence
  • Against higher military budgets and military service+
  • Opposes the €36bn defence top-up and €10bn new aircraft carrier+
  • Rejects protectionism+
  • Refuses 'national unity' behind leaders in current wars+
Institutions

No documented position yet.
